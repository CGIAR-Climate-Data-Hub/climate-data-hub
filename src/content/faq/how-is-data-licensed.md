---
question: How is the data licensed?
order: 3
---

All data in the Hub is required to carry a license to align with FAIR principles. Each record carries the license its producer released it under, often CC-BY-4.0, but not always. The license is shown on every dataset page with a link to the full terms. What you may do with the data follows from that license, so check the one on the dataset you are using. Whichever it is, cite the dataset as given in its citation block.
